HOW TO PLAY

Move: WASD / Arrows  •  Sprint: Shift  •  Pass: X  •  Shoot: hold SPACE, release  •  Tackle: V  •  Switch: Tab  •  Pause: P / Esc

📱 On phones: left stick moves, buttons pass / shoot / tackle / sprint / switch. Hold SHOOT to charge, release to fire — aim with the stick.

🥅 Penalties: aim the marker, hold SHOOT to charge, release. As keeper, tap ◀ ▶ to dive.

🏆 Tournament: 8 clubs, knockout. Draws go to extra time, then penalties.
